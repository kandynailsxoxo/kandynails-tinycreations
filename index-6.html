SHIPPING • PICKUP • DELIVERY

How you'll receive your creation 📦

📦 Shipping

Shipping is available for Tiny Custom Creations. Cost is calculated separately based on order size/weight, packaging, destination and shipping method. Your shipping cost will be confirmed before payment.

📍 Local Pickup

Local pickup can be arranged after your order is finished. Exact pickup details are provided privately after the order is confirmed.

🚗 Local Delivery

Local delivery may be available depending on distance and availability. A delivery fee may apply and will be discussed before the order is finalized.

💜 Tracking & Care

When tracking is available, it will be sent once your order ships. Each handmade creation is packaged carefully. If an item arrives damaged, contact me as soon as possible with photos of the item and packaging.

Creation time and shipping time are separate. Creation is generally 1–3 days for simple requests and 5–14 days for complex requests. Shipping begins after your creation is completed. Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address.
CUSTOM ORDER POLICY

Before I create your order 🖤

Custom pieces are handmade and may have slight variations — that is part of what makes each piece one of a kind.

Pricing is confirmed before work begins. Major changes requested after creation has started may result in an additional charge.

Turnaround is generally 1–3 days for simple creations and 5–14 days for more complex designs, but timing can vary depending on the design and materials needed.

Materials or specialty items may need to be purchased specifically for your request.

Because personalized and custom pieces are made specifically for you, completed custom work is generally not refundable simply because you changed your mind. If there is a problem with your order, please contact me so we can work out an appropriate solution.

💜 Submitting a custom request does not commit you to purchasing anything. We will discuss the design and price first.
