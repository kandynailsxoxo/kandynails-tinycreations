08 · Signature Spa Pedicure

Without Polish — $25

Your health. Your comfort. Your ritual.
Detailed nail and cuticle preparation takes place while your feet are dry, followed by your spa care.

Service illustration · Save this card

Read the appointment steps
  1. Clean & consult: Check your feet and nails, and discuss sensitivities or discomfort before beginning.
  2. The lift: A suitable rounded diamond bit gently removes dead cuticle and surface debris from the nail plate.
  3. The detailer: A suitable tapered bit carefully clears dead cuticle from accessible nail edges without digging into skin.
  4. The polisher: Where appropriate, gently refine loose dead cuticle with a suitable finishing bit. Living skin is left intact.
  5. The final tidy: Trim only loose, detached dead skin as needed. Then shorten and shape the toenails and smooth their edges.
  6. The Epsom soak: Enjoy a comfortably warm foot bath with Epsom salt to soften the skin and begin your spa relaxation.
  7. Smooth down & scrub: Gently smooth rough heels with a disposable foot file, then massage cream scrub over feet and lower legs and rinse.
  8. Deep moisture mask: Apply a rich treatment mask and wrap in comfortably warm towels, following product directions.
  9. Lotion & oil finish: Massage with spa lotion and finish with cuticle oil. No polish is applied.

Your natural finish includes nail preparation, lotion, massage and cuticle oil. Please tell me about any sensitivities or discomfort so I can adjust your service.

11 · Polish & Enhancement Removal

Polish Removal — $5
Gel / Enhancement Removal — from $10

Available without a new set. Product type and removal time affect the final cost, confirmed before starting.

Service illustration · Save this card

Read the appointment steps
  1. Check the existing product: Identify the product where possible and assess the natural nails.
  2. Choose the method: Regular polish uses polish remover. Soak-off products are removed according to their manufacturer instructions. Hard gel is filed down rather than soaked off.
  3. Remove gently: Reduce bulk where appropriate and remove product carefully without pulling or prying. A thin protective layer may be left when appropriate to avoid overfiling the natural nail.
  4. Tidy & finish: Smooth nail edges as needed. When no new product is applied, finish with cuticle oil and lotion.
